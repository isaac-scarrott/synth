# 2026-09-14

## The browser flickered on every click — the theme nudge was resizing the page to a pixel

Clicking in a page still flashed the browser white after the 0.42.1 pane-entrance fix. Recorded
on the running app with a 60fps window capture while Isaac clicked around the storefront harness:
each click that changed the address produced three whole-page white frames, at +0ms, +300ms and
+900ms. That is the retry schedule of `kickPageTheme` — the four-attempt colour-scheme repaint
nudge from 2026-09-06, which `applyPageTheme()` runs on every `addressDidChange`.

The nudge was `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride` at 1×1 followed by `clearDeviceMetricsOverride`.
That is a real resize to one pixel and back, and Chromium paints its background between the two
frames whenever they don't land in the same vsync — which on a page busy laying itself out after a
navigation is most of the time. Sent alone over CDP to the same page, the 1×1 pair blanked the
page for a frame in 2 of 2, then 1 of 3, then 1 of 4 rounds; a click synthesised inside the
renderer (`Input.dispatchMouseEvent`) never did, which is what put the AppKit and CEF sides in the
clear and pointed at the CDP traffic Synth itself sends after a click.

The fix keeps the nudge and takes the resize out of it: `width: 0, height: 0` is CDP's "no size
override", so device emulation is still enabled and cleared — the transition that forces the
layout pass — but the viewport never changes. Measured the same way against the same page: 0 blank
frames in 8 rounds, interleaved with the 1×1 control that still blanked. A same-size override
(`1343×980`) behaved identically, but needs the viewport size and 0×0 doesn't.

Not re-verified: that the 0×0 form still un-sticks the stalled colour-scheme repaint the nudge was
introduced for. That state didn't reproduce during this session, so the claim rests on Chromium
enabling device emulation being the same layout-forcing transition regardless of size. If a page
is seen holding a stale theme after this, that is the thing to test first.

Also ruled out on the way: the 2026-09-08 entrance fix holds (a tab switch is one frame), and the
window's first-responder follower does nothing in a single pane. The frame captures live in the
session's scratch, not the repo.
