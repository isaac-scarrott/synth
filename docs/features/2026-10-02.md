# 2026-10-02

## Synth asks for the microphone

Holding space for voice input in an agent running in a Synth terminal did nothing, and Synth never
appeared under Privacy → Microphone. TCC charges a child process's mic access to the app that
spawned it, and Synth had neither `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` nor, under the hardened runtime,
`com.apple.security.device.audio-input`, so macOS denied without asking. Both are now in the
generated Info.plist and `Synth.entitlements`: the first hold prompts, naming Synth. CEF's helpers
still lack the entitlement, so a web page in the browser pane cannot record yet.

## 0.47.2 ships — Synth asks for the microphone

A patch release carrying the fix above. `CFBundleVersion` 793, tag `v0.47.2`. The first two runs
died at notarization with Apple's 403 "required agreement is missing or has expired"; nothing
shipped until the agreement was accepted. The dmg, the app inside it and the Sparkle zip were each
checked from a quarantined, credential-less download: notarized and stapled, the app inside the
image validates on its own, and the shipped app carries the usage string and the audio-input
entitlement. The appcast is signed on all 18 enclosures; the delta from 0.47.1 is 67 KB. No
`synth-site` push; its links are unchanged.
